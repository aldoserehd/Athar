```
Athar (أثر) is a free, ad-free companion for your daily worship — beautifully simple, private, and fully offline.

No ads. No tracking. No account. No subscriptions. Ever. Built purely for the sake of Allah.

✨ WHAT'S INSIDE
• Accurate prayer times for your exact location, a live countdown to the next prayer, and 12 calculation methods.
• Adhān reminders — pick a reciter for each prayer; a real adhān calls you for every salah, so you know it even from another room.
• Qibla compass — precise direction to the Kaaba, corrected for magnetic declination.
• Salah tracker — log each prayer, keep an honest daily record, and track make-up (qaḍāʾ) prayers.

• Tasbīḥ counter — a digital misbaḥa with targets and a lifetime count.
• The 99 Names of Allah, each with its meaning.
• Hadith library — thousands of authentic, graded narrations from the six books, searchable in Arabic and English, with an optional online deep-search.
• Qiyām, Witr & Sunrise times, plus Duʿāʾ al-Qunūt.
• Prayer Focus — an optional gentle full-screen reminder to put the phone down and pray.
• Inspiring verses and hadith on the virtue of remembrance.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs on your device. Your tracker and settings never leave your phone. No data is collected, and the core features work with no internet connection.

🌙 BILINGUAL & BEYOND
Full English and Arabic with complete right-to-left support — plus Urdu, French, Turkish, Indonesian and Bengali.

Free and ad-free, for the sake of Allah. May He accept it from us all.
```
