Frequently asked questions

Is Athar really free?

Yes. Athar is completely free and ad-free, offered as sadaqah jāriyah. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. The prayer tracker, athkār, qibla and other core tools work without any account and without an internet connection. An optional account will only be needed later to contribute to the community Mosque Wiki.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. We don't upload it. If you delete the app, the data is removed with it — so consider noting your prayer backlog before reinstalling.

Why isn't my adhān making a sound?

Make sure notifications are allowed for Athar in your phone settings, and that your ringer/Do-Not-Disturb isn't silencing it. The custom adhān audio plays in the full app build; if you're testing an early preview, the system notification sound is used instead.

The qibla seems off — what do I do?

Calibrate your phone's compass by moving it in a figure-8 motion, and keep it away from metal or magnets. Athar corrects for magnetic declination automatically once your compass has a good reading.

How do I report a problem with a mosque listing?

Once the Mosque Wiki launches, every listing has a “Report” option. You can also email us directly and we'll review it promptly.

How do I delete my account?

When accounts are available, you'll be able to delete your account and all associated data from within the app's settings. You can also email us to request deletion.
