Frequently asked questions

Is Athar really free?

Yes. Athar is completely free and ad-free, offered as sadaqah jariyah. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. The Qadha tracker and other core tools work without any account and without an internet connection. An optional account will only be needed later to contribute to the community Mosque Wiki.

Where is my Qadha data stored?

Entirely on your device. We don't upload it. If you delete the app, the data is removed with it, so consider noting your backlog before reinstalling.

How is the prayer backlog estimated?

When you set up by time period, Athar applies one missed prayer per day across the span you enter (treating a year as about 354 days). It's a starting estimate — you can fine-tune each prayer's count manually at any time.

How do I report a problem with a mosque listing?

Once the Mosque Wiki launches, every listing has a “Report” option. You can also email us directly and we'll review it promptly.

How do I delete my account?

When accounts are available, you'll be able to delete your account and all associated data from within the app's settings. You can also email us to request deletion.
