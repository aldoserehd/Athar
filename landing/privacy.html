Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

Athar (“Athar”, “we”, “us”) is a free, ad-free app built to serve the Muslim community. We designed it to be private by default. This policy explains, plainly, what data the app handles and what it does not.

The short version: The current version of Athar collects no personal information. Your missed-prayer (Qadha) tracker and your settings are stored only on your device. We run no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account to use the app.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not run third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, some data is saved locally on your phone using the device's standard storage. This never leaves your device unless you explicitly back it up through your own iCloud/Google account:

  • Qadha tracker data — your prayer backlog and the prayers you've logged as made up.
  • Preferences — such as your light/dark theme.

You can remove this data at any time by resetting the tracker in the app or by deleting the app.

3. Features added in future versions

As Athar grows, some features will need additional permissions or services. When they ship, this policy will be updated and the app will ask for your consent in context. Planned features and their data use:

  • Prayer times & qibla — may use your device location on-device to calculate accurate times and direction. Location is not sent to us.
  • Notifications — adhan/prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Mosque Wiki & accounts — to contribute community mosque information you may create an account (email) handled by our backend provider, Supabase. Contributions you submit (e.g. mosque details) are shared publicly with other users by design. You will be able to delete your account and contributions from within the app.

4. Children

Athar is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the current version collects no personal data, no information is gathered from users of any age.

5. Data security

On-device data is protected by your device's own security. Where future features transmit data (e.g. account email), we use encrypted (HTTPS) connections and reputable providers.

6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you today, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our servers. When account features launch, you will be able to access and delete your account data directly in the app, and you may contact us for help at any time.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at salam@athar.app.
